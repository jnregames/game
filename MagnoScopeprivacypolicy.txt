2. Important detection limitations

The app displays readings from a compatible phone's magnetometer and changes relative to a user-calibrated baseline. A magnetic change may be consistent with a nearby magnetic screw, nail or another source. It does not identify the source or confirm a stud, its center, edges, depth, material or load-bearing capacity.

Wood is not directly detected. Some metals and fasteners may not produce a detectable change. Wires, live electrical circuits, pipes, gas or water services, insulation and other hidden hazards may be missed entirely. A quiet reading or absent alert NEVER means an area is clear or safe.

Readings and alerts can be inaccurate, delayed, unstable or unavailable. Sensor quality and location, phone orientation, movement, calibration, background magnetic fields, wall thickness, accessories, magnetic cases, nearby electronics, vibration and flashlight operation can affect results. Sensitivity settings, peak markers, saved comparisons and reports do not establish accuracy or professional reliability. Demo mode produces simulated data and must not be used for real-world decisions. Watching an ad unlocks convenience features only; it does not improve sensor accuracy or provide a safety certification.

4. Information processed on your device

Magnetic readings are processed locally by the app. We do not operate a server that receives your raw magnetic readings, scan names, bookmarks or reports. The app itself does not request precise location, microphone, contacts or photo-library access, and does not require an account.

Your acknowledgement version, language and acceptance timestamp are stored locally. Language, sensitivity and alert preferences and temporary rewarded-feature access are stored on the device. If you choose to save a scan, its name, timestamp, duration, baseline, peak change and manually marked readings are stored in app-private storage, with up to 20 saved scans and a local backup of the previous archive. Avoid including personal or sensitive details in scan names. Standard device backup services, if enabled, may also back up app data under your device settings.

Sharing is initiated only when you select Share report. Android then lets you choose a recipient or app. On editor/desktop test versions, the report is copied to the clipboard. The service you choose handles the shared copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

7. Rewarded and interstitial ads

Basic visualization and alert configuration are free. An optional rewarded ad grants 30 minutes of Survey Toolkit access only after the ad provider confirms completion. It has no cash value, is stored locally, and does not increase detection capability. Saved reports remain accessible after access expires. Ads may be unavailable, fail to load or be restricted by privacy choices or connectivity.

Interstitials are limited to breaks after the user finishes qualifying scans, not during active scanning. Survey Toolkit access suppresses interstitials while active. Ad content and linked destinations are provided by third parties; opening them is your choice.
